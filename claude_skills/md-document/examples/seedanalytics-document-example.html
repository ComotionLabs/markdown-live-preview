Analytics Maturity Assessment

Seed Analytics provides data strategy and analytics implementation services to help organisations grow.

Assessment Framework

We evaluate organisations across five dimensions: data governance, technology stack, skills, processes, and culture.

Scoring Methodology

Each dimension is scored from 1 (ad hoc) to 5 (optimised). The overall maturity level is the weighted average.

Findings Summary

Dimension Score Priority
Data governance 3.2 Medium
Technology stack 2.8 High
Skills 3.5 Low
Processes 2.5 High
Culture 3.0 Medium

Recommendations

  1. Technology consolidation — Reduce tool sprawl and standardise on a core platform.
  2. Process documentation — Formalise data workflows and quality checks.
  3. Upskill team — Invest in analytics training for business users.
