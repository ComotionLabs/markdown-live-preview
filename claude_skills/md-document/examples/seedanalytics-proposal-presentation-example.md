# Analytics Enablement Programme
Proposal for Acme Corp | Seed Analytics

---

## Objective

- **Unify** reporting across Finance, Sales, and Operations
- **Establish** a single source of truth for key metrics
- **Enable** self-serve analytics for 50+ business users within 12 months

---

## Scope

| Workstream | Deliverables | Duration |
|------------|---------------|----------|
| Discovery and design | Data model, KPI catalogue, UX wireframes | 6 weeks |
| Build and integrate | Pipelines, semantic layer, dashboards | 14 weeks |
| Rollout and adoption | Training, support, governance | 8 weeks |

---

## Approach

- **Phased delivery** — Finance first (Q2), then Sales and Operations (Q3–Q4)
- **Agile sprints** — Two-week cycles with demos and feedback
- **Governance from day one** — Definitions, ownership, and change control
