# Analytics Roadmap 2025
Priorities and milestones

---

## Roadmap at a Glance

- **Q1** — Governance and foundations (data catalogue, quality rules)
- **Q2** — Self-serve reporting (Finance and Sales)
- **Q3** — Advanced analytics (forecasting, segmentation)
- **Q4** — Scale and optimise (performance, adoption)

> Set the scene: "We're going to walk through the four quarters and what each means for the business."

---

## Q1: Foundations

| Initiative | Owner | Target |
|------------|-------|--------|
| Data catalogue | Data team | End Feb |
| Quality rules and monitoring | Analytics | End Mar |
| Governance policy rollout | Compliance | Apr |

> Emphasise that Q1 is mostly invisible to users but critical. "No flashy dashboards yet — we're building the plumbing."

---

## Q2: Self-Serve Reporting

- **Finance** — P&L, variance, and management packs in the new platform
- **Sales** — Pipeline, win/loss, and territory views
- **Training** — Two cohorts of power users; documentation and office hours

> If they ask about "old reports": "We'll run legacy and new in parallel for one quarter, then sunset legacy with plenty of notice."
